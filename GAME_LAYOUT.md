# Shadow of the Ember Clan — game layout

## Story

The warlord Akuro has seized five ember seals from the mountain shrines. His Hollow Forge will bind their fire into an army at dawn. Ren, a surviving courier of the Ember Clan, returns through the flooded harbour to recover the seals and break the furnace's bond. Sora, a travelling shrine keeper, leaves clues along the route. Breaking each seal frees one part of the province. The dawn deadline is narrative, not a campaign timer.

## Campaign

| Region | Environment and encounter | Puzzle clue and order | Guardian | Story payoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. The Drowned Harbour | Lantern-lit quay, moored boats, warehouses, rain; sword guards and archers | Open sluices from sea to shore: II, I, III | Harbour Warden | Water recedes; the inland road opens |
| 2. The Whispering Bamboo | Jade bamboo, moss paths, shrine roofs, fireflies; skirmishers | Follow the wind: III, I, II | Reed Stalker | The forest's warning bells fall silent |
| 3. The Bell Monastery | Snowy stone terraces, frozen pools, prayer flags; spear monks | Ring from earth to sky: I, II, III | Silent Abbot | The stolen monastery seal is released |
| 4. The Hollow Forge | Iron floor, lava channels, furnaces, chains and sparks; armoured guards | Cool the heart before its veins: II, III, I | Furnace Keeper | The furnace loses its source of power |
| 5. The Ember Citadel | Vermilion courtyards, burning rooftops, ash; mixed elite defenders | Remember the clan's oath: III, II, I | Akuro, the Ash Regent | Break the last seal; dawn returns |

Each map has three relics at marked world locations, three numbered puzzle nodes, one save shrine, a guide, patrolling enemies, and a guardian altar. Relics can be collected in any order. Puzzle nodes must be activated in the clue's order; a wrong step resets only the puzzle. The altar explains missing objectives and begins the guardian encounter only when ready. Interact again after victory to travel onward.

## Flow and checkpoints

Loading homage → title → optional intro → harbour → bamboo → monastery → forge → citadel → ending → credits → title.

Start every level with an initial save. Interacting with its shrine heals and writes a new checkpoint including recovered relics, defeated enemies, current puzzle state and supplies. A death offers retry at that checkpoint. Continue restores the last checkpoint after closing/reloading. Normal pause does not save unfinished progress. Travelling to the next region saves the new region immediately.

## Controls

- WASD / arrow keys: move relative to the screen.
- J / left mouse / gamepad X: sword; K / right mouse / gamepad B: throw star.
- Space / gamepad A: dodge; Shift / gamepad left trigger: guard.
- E / gamepad Y: interact; Esc / P / gamepad Start: pause.
- M: toggle music. Touch controls expose movement and the same actions.

## Sprite and sound inventory

Player Ren; Sora; sword guard; spear guard; archer; armoured guard; five guardian colour/silhouette variants. Every combatant uses the shared direction and animation system. Props include three relics, numbered puzzle nodes, checkpoint shrine, altar, shuriken, arrows, hit particles and healing effects.

Separate original melodic patterns for all five levels, plus title/intro and ending modes. Guardian mode increases urgency. Sword, guard, projectile, injury, pickup, puzzle error/success, save, transition and victory cues have distinct synthesized sounds.

## Intro and credits

Animated rain, embers and subtle layered movement over the approved intro illustration. Four short captions establish the stolen seals, the forge, the mission and the road inland. Skip is always available.

Ending shows dawn over the mountain and resolves Ren's journey.
