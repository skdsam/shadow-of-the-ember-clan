# Shadow of the Ember Clan — visual proposal

Status: initial concept for review, 23 October 2026. Working title.

These three images were generated with the built-in image generation tool. They are visual targets, not runtime screenshots or production sprite sheets. Their apparent palette and pixel grid are illustrative; production assets will need an enforced palette, resolution, consistent animation and readable collision silhouettes.

- `01-loading-screen.png`: portrait, title, moonlit citadel and cassette-style border.
- `02-harbour-gameplay.png`: fixed isometric view, player, spear guard, archer, inventory and checkpoint shrine.
- `03-cinematic-intro.png`: layered rooftop scene establishing the citadel and five seals.

## Proposed game

An original ninja action adventure. An occupying warlord has stolen five ember seals to awaken a furnace beneath the mountain. A survivor of the Ember Clan must cross the occupied province, recover and break each seal, and stop the awakening before dawn. The dawn deadline is initially a narrative device, not a global gameplay timer.

Five playable levels: The Drowned Harbour; The Whispering Bamboo; The Bell Monastery; The Hollow Forge; The Ember Citadel. Each should have a distinct palette, original musical theme, exploration puzzle, enemy introduction, checkpoint and concluding encounter.

Pixel sprites must cover the player, enemies, bosses and supporting characters, with consistent direction sets and idle, movement, attack, hit and defeat animations. Exact sizes and frame budgets will be established in the build plan after visual approval.

Original music direction: SID-inspired bass, pulse-wave arpeggios, pentatonic leads, noise percussion and synthesized bell/plucked timbres. Plan an intro/loader theme, five level loops, boss cue, ending and credits theme. No existing Last Ninja recordings or melodies are proposed.

Presentation: skippable cassette-style loading sequence, animated layered intro, title/options, level transitions, ending and credits with accurate human, tool and asset attribution.

## Research

- Original manual: https://apple2online.com/wp-content/uploads/Last-Ninja-Manual.pdf — combat, inventory, hazards and original team credits.
- System 3 soundtrack history: https://shop.system3.com/products/the-last-ninja-mug-s3 — atmospheric music and the contributions of Anthony Lees and Ben Daglish.
- SID tune metadata: https://www.slayradio.org/sidinfo/MUSICIANS/D/Daglish_Ben/Last_Ninja.sid — loading and gameplay music references.

The concept takes inspiration from isometric exploration, readable combat encounters and strong musical atmosphere while proposing original characters, environments, story and music.
